About Your Life Care Planner

Christiane Pittaluga, BSN, RN, JD, CLCP

Christiane Pittaluga brings a rare combination to life care planning: a Registered Nurse who has delivered hands-on patient care, an attorney who has litigated medical malpractice and motor vehicle liability cases, and a Certified Life Care Planner who translates catastrophic injury into the concrete, defensible numbers a case requires.

As a home health care case manager, Christiane provided direct care and case management to homebound patients — sourcing medical equipment for patients with paraplegia and fractures, collaborating with physicians on medication cost savings, and providing wound care for ulcers and traumatic injuries. That bedside experience means every plan reflects what injured people actually need, day after day, for the rest of their lives.

  • Certified Life Care Planner (CLCP)
  • Registered Nurse — BSN, Georgetown University School of Nursing
  • Juris Doctor — Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law
  • RN licensure — Florida (Multistate)
  • Member, The Florida Bar and New York Bar
  • Former medical malpractice and motor vehicle liability litigator
  • Deposition and trial testimony experience — nationwide
Why “Invictus”? Invictus is Latin for unconquered. A catastrophic injury changes a life — it does not have to conquer it. Every life care plan prepared here is dedicated to one goal: restoring quality of life, with every future need identified, priced, and provided for.
